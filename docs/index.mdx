BibGenie is a **Zotero AI plugin** for reading papers, searching your library, and organizing references. [**Paragraph-level citations and source highlighting**](/docs/bibgenie-citations) help you trace conclusions back to evidence and examine assumptions and limitations. Use built-in models or connect [your own API key or local model](/docs/custom-models).

Work in Zotero’s sidebar or a separate window. Ask about the paper you are reading or the items you have selected, or add PDFs, EPUBs, snapshots, notes, annotations, and images. Connect [**Notion**](/docs/zotero-to-notion) to read or update authorized workspace content during conversations.

Chat history is stored locally in Zotero; where research content is processed depends on the models and connected services you use.

New to BibGenie? [Install the plugin and ask your first question](/docs/getting-started).

## Research Workflow

1. Describe your research goal and the papers, collection, or other material you want to work with.
2. Ask about the paper you are reading or the items selected in Zotero, [add specific context](/docs/add-context), or ask BibGenie to search your library. You can summarize one paper, compare several, or ask a follow-up in the same conversation.
3. [Follow citations back to the sources](/docs/bibgenie-citations) to check important claims. Before approving library changes, review the affected items and proposed edits.
4. Refine the result with a follow-up or [save it as a Zotero note](/docs/save-as-note). [Quick Prompts](/docs/quick-prompts) provides examples you can adapt.
