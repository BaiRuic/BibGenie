You can choose how citations look in BibGenie chat: compact numbers or readable source labels. Both let you return to the supporting source.

## Choose How Citations Appear

1. Open BibGenie **Settings → Chat Settings**.
2. Find **Citation Appearance**.
3. Select **Numbered** or **Source labels**. The **Chat preview** shows the difference.

| Option | What you see | When it helps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Numbered | Short markers such as `[1]` and `[2]` | Keep answers compact while reading |
| Source labels | Readable labels identifying the source | Recognize sources while checking an answer |

This preference applies to citations across your chats. It changes their appearance, not the evidence or source location behind them. You do not need to regenerate an answer to change the display preference.

## Copying Answers and Saving Notes

Copied answers and saved Zotero notes use readable source citations, even when you choose numbered markers in chat. This keeps source information understandable outside the conversation.

For saving an answer, see [Save as Note](/docs/save-as-note). For supported sources and what happens when you select a citation, see [Traceable AI Answers](/docs/bibgenie-citations).

## What This Setting Does Not Change

- It does not select an APA, MLA, Chicago, or other manuscript citation style.
- It does not change Zotero's Word plugin, bibliography settings, or item metadata.
- A source label is not proof that an answer is correct. Open the source and check whether it supports the claim.

For a paper or report, use Zotero's normal citation workflow and the style required by your journal or institution.
